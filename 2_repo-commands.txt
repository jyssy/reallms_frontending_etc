• The guarded workflow does not support writable access to two repositories in one plan. Use two plan/approve/execute cycles:

1. Instrument the orchestrator, with the Django repo as read-only context.
2. Update Django, with the modified orchestrator as read-only context.

This preserves integration order and the one-writer-per-repository rule.
